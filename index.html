The Physics

She floats when the water she pushes aside weighs more than the crew.

Freshwater weighs 62.4 pounds per cubic foot. Add both paddlers plus your safety margin, divide by that density, and you have the volume the hull must sink to displace. The rest is trim.

Transverse Stability

Will she tip over sideways?

Length-to-beam is a proxy. This is the real number navy architects use: metacentric height (GM) — how far the boat's weight sits below the point it actually pivots around.

KB = height of the center of buoyancy above the keel
BM = I(transverse waterplane inertia) / V(displaced volume)
KG = height of the combined crew + hull center of gravity
GM = KB + BM − KG  →  positive GM rights itself, negative GM stays tipped
